Finding intimate images of yourself online, real or fake, can feel like the ground has gone from under you. Whatever you are feeling right now is a normal reaction to something that was done **to** you.

## Common feelings

- **Shock or numbness.** It may not feel real.
- **Shame.** You may want to hide. Remember: the shame belongs to the person who shared it, not to you.
- **Fear** about who has seen it, or what happens next.
- **Anger**, at the person who did it or at the platforms.
- **Trouble sleeping, eating or concentrating.**

## Small steps that help

1. **Breathe.** Slow your breathing: in for four counts, out for six. Do it a few times.
2. **Tell one person you trust.** You don't have to explain everything.
3. **Do one practical thing.** Saving a link or reporting one post is progress.
4. **Take breaks from looking.** You don't need to keep checking. Ask a friend to watch for new posts.
5. **Look after your body.** Water, food and rest matter more than usual right now.

## When to get more help

If you feel overwhelmed, can't stop thinking about it, or feel unsafe, please reach out:

- **Tele-MANAS: 14416** (or 1-800-891-4416), free, confidential, 24x7, in many Indian languages.
- **Women Helpline: 181**, which can also connect you to a One Stop Centre.
- **Emergency: 112**, if you are in danger.

## You are not alone

This happens to many people across India, of every background. You did not cause it, and you can act today.
