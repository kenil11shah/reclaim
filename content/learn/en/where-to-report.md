MeitY's Standard Operating Procedure on NCII (November 2025) lists the official ways to report. You can use more than one at the same time.

## 1. The platform

Use the in-app **Report** button or the platform's web form. Pick the option about nudity, sexual content or private images. Write down the time and any reference number.

If nothing happens within 2 hours, write to the platform's **India Grievance Officer**. Reclaim's platform guides list the exact steps for Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, X, Reddit, YouTube and Google Search.

## 2. The National Cybercrime Reporting Portal

Go to **cybercrime.gov.in** and choose the section for crimes against women and children. This creates an official record with the police cyber cell, and the government's I4C keeps a secure "hash bank" of reported NCII to help stop re-uploads.

You can also call **1930**, the National Cybercrime Helpline.

## 3. One Stop Centres

**One Stop Centres** (also called Sakhi centres) help in one place: filing on NCRP, legal support, counselling and medical help. Call **181**, the Women Helpline, to find the nearest one.

## 4. The police

You can file a complaint or FIR at your local police station. Take your evidence log (links, usernames, dates) and Reclaim's police letter. You do not have to show the images themselves.

## 5. Search engines

Even after a site removes something, copies can appear in search results. Ask Google to remove them using "It shows a sexual image of me".

## Which order?

Reclaim's action plan suggests: save evidence, report on each platform, file on NCRP, escalate if a platform misses the 2-hour deadline, and then use StopNCII and Google removal.
