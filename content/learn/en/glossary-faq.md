## Glossary

- **NCII**: non-consensual intimate imagery. Intimate images shared without consent.
- **Deepfake**: an image, video or voice made or changed by AI to look real.
- **Morphed image**: a picture edited to put someone's face on another body.
- **Nudify app or bot**: a tool that creates a fake nude from a normal photo.
- **Sextortion**: blackmail using intimate images, real or fake.
- **Hash**: a digital fingerprint of an image, used to block re-uploads without sharing the image.
- **NCRP**: National Cybercrime Reporting Portal, cybercrime.gov.in.
- **I4C**: Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre, under the Ministry of Home Affairs.
- **Grievance Officer**: the person every large platform must appoint to handle complaints from India.
- **GAC**: Grievance Appellate Committee, where you can appeal a Grievance Officer's decision.
- **SGI**: "synthetically generated information", the legal term for AI-made content in the IT Rules.
- **FIR**: First Information Report, the police record that starts a criminal case.

## FAQ

**Does Reclaim see my information?**
No. Reclaim has no server database and no accounts. What you type is stored only in your browser, and "Delete everything" removes it.

**Do I need to upload the image?**
Never. Reclaim never asks for, uploads or stores any image.

**Does Reclaim file complaints for me?**
No. It drafts the text. You paste it on the official site yourself, so you stay in control.

**Is this legal advice?**
No. Reclaim explains the law in plain words with sources. For advice on your case, contact a lawyer, a One Stop Centre or your District Legal Services Authority.

**Is it free?**
Yes.

**What if someone sees Reclaim on my phone?**
Use the "Leave now" button or press Esc. You can also turn on a neutral tab title and a PIN in Privacy settings.
