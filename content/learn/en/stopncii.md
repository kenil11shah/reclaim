## What it does

**StopNCII.org** is a free tool for adults whose intimate images have been shared, or might be. You choose the image or video on your phone or computer. StopNCII turns it into a **hash**: a code, like a digital fingerprint, that represents the image.

Only the hash is sent. StopNCII says your images **never leave your device** and are never saved by them. Partner platforms then use the hash to spot and block that image if someone tries to upload it.

## Who can use it?

- You must be **over 18 in the image**.
- The image must be of you and intimate (nude or sexual).
- You need to have the image on your device.
- It works for AI-made images of you too.

If the image was taken when you were under 18, use **Take It Down** by NCMEC instead.

## Which platforms take part?

Partners include Facebook, Instagram, Reddit and X, among others. Google announced a partnership for Search in September 2025. Check the StopNCII partner page for the latest list.

## Its limits

- **Encrypted messaging apps** like WhatsApp and Telegram chats are not covered, because the platform cannot see the content.
- It only matches the **same or very similar** image. A heavily edited copy may not match.
- It **prevents re-uploads**; it does not remove content that is already up. Report existing posts separately.
- Websites that are not partners do not use the hashes.

## Should I use it?

If you have the image and you are over 18, it is a good extra layer of protection. It works alongside reporting, not instead of it.
