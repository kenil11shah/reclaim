**Myth: "If you sent the photo, it's your fault."**
Fact: Sharing an image with one person is not consent for anyone else to see it. The person who passes it on breaks the law. BNS section 77 specifically covers sharing images that were agreed to be taken but not shared.

**Myth: "It's fake, so it doesn't matter."**
Fact: Fake intimate images cause real harm, and the law covers "artificially morphed" images. Platforms must remove them within 2 hours of your complaint.

**Myth: "Only celebrities get deepfaked."**
Fact: Research on one large deepfake site found hundreds of targets with little or no public presence. One clear photo is enough.

**Myth: "Reporting is pointless."**
Fact: Platforms do remove content, and since February 2026 they have a legal deadline. If they miss it, you can escalate. Keeping a record of what you reported and when makes escalation much stronger.

**Myth: "Going to the police means everyone will find out."**
Fact: You can file online at cybercrime.gov.in, and you can also get help through a One Stop Centre. You decide who you tell.

**Myth: "If I pay the blackmailer, it will stop."**
Fact: Paying usually leads to more demands. Save the evidence, block, and report.

**Myth: "Once it's online, it's there forever."**
Fact: It can be hard to remove everything, but you can get it taken down from platforms, removed from Google Search, and blocked from re-uploads with StopNCII.

**Myth: "I should search for it everywhere myself."**
Fact: Looking again and again can be very painful. Ask someone you trust to help look and note the links.
