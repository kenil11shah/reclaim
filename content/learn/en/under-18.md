If the person in the image or video is **under 18**, or was under 18 when it was taken, it needs special care.

## The most important rule

**Do not save, forward or screenshot the content**, even to show the police or a parent. Possessing or sharing sexual images of a child is a crime, even when you mean to help. Write down only:

- the link (URL)
- the account username
- the platform
- the date and time you saw it

## Where to report

- **cybercrime.gov.in**: the portal has a section for crimes against children.
- **1930**: the National Cybercrime Helpline.
- **1098**: the Child Helpline, 24x7, integrated with the 112 emergency system.
- **Take It Down** (takeitdown.ncmec.org): like StopNCII, but for images taken when someone was under 18. It works on the device without uploading the image.

## If you are a parent, teacher or friend

- **Stay calm and listen.** Your reaction decides whether they keep talking to you.
- **Tell them it's not their fault**, even if they sent the image themselves.
- **Don't take away their phone as punishment.** It can make them hide future problems.
- **Get help from the routes above** rather than handling it alone.

## If you are under 18

You have not done anything wrong by asking for help. Talk to an adult you trust, or call 1098. If someone is blackmailing you, stop replying, don't send anything more, and report.

## Why Reclaim stops here

Reclaim's normal plan is built for adults. For anyone under 18, it shows only official child-safety routes, because these cases are handled under the POCSO Act and need specialists.
