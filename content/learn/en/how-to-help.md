When someone tells you their images have been shared, how you react in the first few minutes matters a lot.

## Do

- **Believe them.** Say "This is not your fault" and mean it.
- **Ask what they want.** Some people want to act straight away. Others need time. It is their decision.
- **Offer practical help.** You can collect links and usernames so they don't have to look at the content again and again.
- **Sit with them** while they report, or read the steps out loud.
- **Check in later.** The shock often hits days after.

## Don't

- **Don't forward, download or screenshot the content** to show anyone, including family or friends. Each copy makes it harder to control.
- **Don't confront the person** who did it. It can make things worse and put you both at risk.
- **Don't blame.** Questions like "Why did you send it?" or "Why is your profile public?" add to the shame.
- **Don't post about it** publicly without their permission, even to support them.
- **Don't promise outcomes**, like "It'll be gone by tonight". Platforms are often slow.

## Collecting evidence for them

Use Reclaim's evidence kit. Write down the link, the account handle, the platform and the date and time. Store it privately. If the person shown might be **under 18**, do not screenshot anything; write down the link and use the child-safety routes.

## Look after yourself

Helping someone through this is hard. It's okay to feel angry or upset. Tele-MANAS (14416) is there for helpers too.
